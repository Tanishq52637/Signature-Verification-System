What is signature verification?

Signature verification is a biometric authentication task. We classify signatures as Genuine or Forged using extracted image features. Two approaches: Offline (image-based — this system) and Online (pen speed + pressure in real time). Forgery types: Random, Simple, and Skilled (hardest to detect).
